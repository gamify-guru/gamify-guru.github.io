Grammar Rules

1. Affirmative
verb + ed
play → played   visit → visited   tidy → tidied
carry → carried   stop → stopped

2. Negative
didn’t + verb (base form)
He didn’t play the drums.
She didn’t visit Grandma.

3. Questions
Did + subject + verb (base form)?
Did you watch TV?
Did Mum cook dinner?

Time words: yesterday, last night, last weekend, ago
